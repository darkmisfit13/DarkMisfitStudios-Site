About Benjamin James

Founder, creator, and maker of OmniAI Hub.

I’m Benjamin James, founder and content creator at DarkMisfitStudios and creator of OmniAI Hub. I studied computer skills and web page design at Mountain Empire Community College.

My work brings AI tools, prompt engineering, and hands-on editing together to make visual art, music videos, image restorations, AI-assisted music, and poetry. I’m drawn to the raw emotions—heartbreak, loss, betrayal, and rebirth—that give creative work its pulse.
